# Pi Rollups medal artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool. These are new minimal designs replacing the initial detailed medal concept. The site uses transparent WebP assets, resized to 256 × 256 for display at 38–100 CSS pixels.

## Files

- bronze.webp — Bronze, awarded at 1 win
- silver.webp — Silver, awarded at 5 wins
- gold.webp — Gold, awarded at 10 wins
- platinum.webp — Platinum, awarded at 30 wins
- chaos.webp — Chaos honor, accompanied by the cumulative victory count

## Generation prompt

Create a brand-new minimal modern achievement icon for Pi Rollups, a dark olive math arcade. Flat vector-like raster graphic, two or three solid colors ONLY. A large simple Greek pi (π) symbol centered inside a solid geometric badge with one thin inset circular outline. Front facing, perfectly centered on a square canvas. Occupies 72% of canvas. True transparent background (alpha). NO ribbon, NO laurel leaves, NO ornament, NO texture, NO bevel, NO metal reflection, NO gradient, NO shadow, NO words or numerals. Clean silhouette legible at 32px.

Append the following variant instruction to the prompt above:

- Bronze: Circular disc. Muted warm copper bronze #B87B4B fill, lighter copper inset ring #E1B18C, dark brown pi #362A20.
- Silver: Circular disc. Cool silver #B9C3C7 fill, pale silver inset ring #E3E9E9, dark slate pi #303C42.
- Gold: Circular disc. Warm gold #D8B653 fill, pale gold inset ring #F6DE9A, dark brown pi #42351D.
- Platinum: Circular disc. Icy platinum #B5DDDF fill, pale ice inset ring #E4F5F5, dark teal pi #24575C.
- Chaos: A simple symmetrical regular eight-point star (equal points) instead of circular disc. Burnt orange #DD8D48 fill, light amber inset circle #F4C293, dark brown pi #422B20.
